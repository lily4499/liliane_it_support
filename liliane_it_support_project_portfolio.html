Help Desk & Ticketing · Workstation support

The Loaner Turnaround

Completed simulated support ticket
Business problem

A shared laptop was not ready for its next user. Several independent issues created connection, sign-in, and application problems.

My objective

Return the device to a reliable, usable state and document proof for each repair.

How I approached it

  1. 01
    Triage the symptoms

    Separated network, startup, browser, and sign-in issues instead of assuming one root cause.

  2. 02
    Restore connectivity

    Reinstalled the Intel Wi-Fi 6 AX201 driver and verified that network adapters appeared correctly.

  3. 03
    Remove startup and browser blockers

    Disabled unnecessary startup items and the outbound firewall rule that blocked msedge.exe.

  4. 04
    Restore authorized access

    Re-enabled the intended loaner-user account and confirmed a successful sign-in.

Verified result / deliverable

Verified both adapters, restored secure-site access and user login, and recorded an 8-second sign-in after startup cleanup.

Why it matters to a business

Less time lost at device handoff and clearer support-ticket resolution.
